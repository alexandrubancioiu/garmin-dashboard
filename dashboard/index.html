      How the numbers are worked out
      Readiness
      If your watch computes Training Readiness, the dashboard shows Garmin's score. Otherwise it shows its own estimate: a weighted average of five components, each on a 0 to 100 scale: sleep score (25%), last night's HRV against its 7-day average (25%), the day's highest Body Battery (20%), resting heart rate against its 14-day median (15%) and form (15%). Above 75 means ready for a hard day, under 55 an easy or rest day. The estimated components are shown either way.
      Fitness, fatigue and form
      Fitness is the 42-day exponential average of daily training load, fatigue the same average over 7 days. Today's form is yesterday's fitness minus yesterday's fatigue. Between -10 and -30 you are in a solid training block; above +5 you are fresh.
      Training load
      The value Garmin computes for each activity from its cardiac effort (EPOC). Days without activity have a load of 0.
      Aerobic efficiency
      A run's average speed in metres per minute divided by its average heart rate.
